A2 About the Practice

A structural engineering partner for Mumbai's built environment

Archstone Consulting Engineers is a Mumbai-based structural engineering consultancy serving residential societies, commercial establishments, industrial facilities and public institutions across the MMR region — from first inspection through to certification.

We pair engineering rigour with on-site experience and plain-language reporting, so clients can act on our findings with confidence. Alongside ACE, we operate Stanchion Designs Pvt Ltd, extending our reach across a broader range of engineering and project-related services.

Vision

To be a trusted engineering partner delivering durable, safe and economic structural solutions that extend the lifespan and safety of buildings.

Mission
  • Accurate, unbiased structural audits and sound engineering judgment
  • Safe, economical design for RCC, steel, load-bearing and composite structures
  • Retrofitting strategies that improve performance and extend service life
  • Repair methodologies suited to Mumbai's climate and building stock
  • Ethical, transparent and timely service on every engagement
